# Active Protection Systems

**Active Protection Systems** (APS) intercept incoming rockets and missiles before they hit the vehicle. The APS was part of ITC Land Systems Redux and is now its own mod.

# Requirements

- [CBA_A3](https://github.com/CBATeam/CBA_A3)
- [ACE3](https://github.com/acemod/ACE3)

# Features

- **Trophy, Drozd and Afghanit** on the vanilla main battle tanks (Slammer, Varsuk, Angara)
- **Per-module arcs, range, shots and kill chance**; the arcs turn with the turret
- **Multiplayer-safe**: ammo and reload state stay consistent with several shooters
- **Fit an APS to any vehicle** through its config
- **CBA setting** to switch the APS off server-wide
